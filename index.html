About Us

We have 2 club nights open to everyone Mondays and Thursdays 7pm to 9:30pm.

We have a women-only club night on Tuesdays 7pm to 9:30pm as well.

For beginners we'll be hosting improver sessions at the start of year, so feel free to come along and learn something new!
For competitive players we field 2 teams in the local Birmingham league, with the second team being targeted at people new to league chess and longer time controls.

In addition, we have a joint partnership with the University of Warwick's Chess Club to field a 4NCL team in Division 4 with weekend fixtures in the Midlands.

We also host frequent social pub trips after chess to the Soak, an amazing pub located on Bristol Road
